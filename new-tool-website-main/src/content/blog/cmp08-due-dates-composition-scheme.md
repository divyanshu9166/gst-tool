The GST Composition Scheme was introduced to significantly reduce the compliance burden for small businesses. Instead of filing monthly GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B returns, composition taxpayers file a simple quarterly statement known as **Form CMP-08**.

However, fewer returns do not mean you can ignore the [Due Dates Calendar](/gst-due-dates). Missing the CMP-08 deadline carries penalties, though the structure differs slightly from regular taxpayers.

## What is Form CMP-08?

Form CMP-08 is a "Statement for payment of self-assessed tax." 
Unlike regular taxpayers who compute complex Input Tax Credit (ITC) equations, composition taxpayers pay a flat percentage of their turnover out of their own pocket (they cannot collect GST from customers, nor can they claim ITC).

- **Traders and Manufacturers**: **1%** of turnover (0.5% CGST + 0.5% SGST)
- **Restaurants (not serving alcohol)**: **5%** of turnover (2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST)
- **Service Providers**: **6%** of turnover (3% CGST + 3% SGST)

*(Note: Since you cannot charge GST, you do not need to use a [GST Calculator](/gst-calculator) to add tax to your invoices. Your invoices must be titled "Bill of Supply" instead of "Tax Invoice").*

## The CMP-08 Quarterly Filing Calendar (FY 2025-26 & 2026-27)

CMP-08 must be filed every quarter by the 18th of the month following the quarter's end:

| Quarter | Period Covered | Statutory Due Date | What Needs to be Paid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Q1** | April 1 – June 30 | **July 18** | Self-assessed tax on Q1 turnover |
| **Q2** | July 1 – September 30 | **October 18** | Self-assessed tax on Q2 turnover |
| **Q3** | October 1 – December 31 | **January 18** | Self-assessed tax on Q3 turnover |
| **Q4** | January 1 – March 31 | **April 18** | Self-assessed tax on Q4 turnover |

In addition to quarterly CMP-08, composition dealers must file an annual return in **Form GSTR-4** by **April 30th** following the close of the financial year.

## Worked Example: Calculating CMP-08 Quarterly Tax

Suppose a retail trader registered under the composition scheme has the following figures for Q1 (April to June):
- Total taxable sales turnover: **₹15,00,000**
- Inward supplies attracting RCM (e.g. transport freight): **₹50,000** @ 5% GST = ₹2,500

### Tax Liability Computation:
1. **Composition Tax on Outward Sales (1%)**: 
   - CGST (0.5%): ₹7,500
   - SGST (0.5%): ₹7,500
   - Subtotal = **₹15,000**
2. **Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM) Liability**:
   - CGST (2.5%): ₹1,250
   - SGST (2.5%): ₹1,250
   - Subtotal = **₹2,500** *(Note: RCM must be paid at standard applicable rate in cash, not composition rate)*
3. **Total Cash Payable in CMP-08**: **₹17,500**

## Penalties and Interest for Late CMP-08

### 1. Late Fees
Historically, the GST portal did not automatically calculate a per-day late fee on filing CMP-08 late (unlike GSTR-3B). However, any delay is accounted for when filing the annual return (**Form GSTR-4**). The late fee for GSTR-4 is capped at ₹500 for NIL returns and ₹2,000 for regular returns.

### 2. Mandatory 18% Interest Under Section 50
If you miss the 18th deadline and fail to deposit the self-assessed tax, you must pay **18% per annum interest** on the delayed payment amount. Since composition dealers cannot claim ITC, the entire tax amount is paid in cash, meaning the 18% is calculated on the full net liability from the day after the due date until the date of payment.

## Crucial Restrictions

Remember, as a composition dealer:
1. You cannot make inter-state sales of goods (you can only sell within your state).
2. You may supply goods through an e-commerce operator under the special procedure in [Notification No. 36/2023-Central Tax](https://www.gstcouncil.gov.in/node/4391). The operator must block inter-state supplies, collect TCS, and report the sales; check the marketplace's current onboarding requirements.
3. You must mention "Composition taxable person, not eligible to collect tax on supplies" on every Bill of Supply.

Always perform a [GSTIN Validator](/gstin-validator) check on your suppliers, even though you can't claim ITC, to ensure you are dealing with legitimate businesses. Keeping track of your [GST Due Dates](/gst-due-dates) ensures you enjoy the benefits of the composition scheme without the headache of notices.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the due date of Form CMP-08?
Form CMP-08 is due by the 18th of the month following each quarter: 18th July (Q1), 18th October (Q2), 18th January (Q3), and 18th April (Q4).

### CMP-08 late fee kitna lagta hai agar date nikal jaye?
CMP-08 par monthly filing ke time portal automatic late fee nahi lagata, lekin quarterly tax par **18% p.a. interest (Section 50)** mandatory lagta hai. Saal ke ant me annual return GSTR-4 file karte waqt late fee absorb hoti hai, jo regular returns ke liye maximum ₹2,000 tak capped hai.

### Can I file a NIL CMP-08 statement?
Yes. If you had zero turnover and zero inward supplies subject to RCM in a quarter, you can file a Nil Form CMP-08 directly on the GST portal or via SMS.

### What is the difference between CMP-08 and GSTR-4?
CMP-08 is a quarterly tax payment statement filed 4 times a year to deposit tax. GSTR-4 is the comprehensive annual return for composition taxpayers filed once a year by 30th April following the close of the financial year.
