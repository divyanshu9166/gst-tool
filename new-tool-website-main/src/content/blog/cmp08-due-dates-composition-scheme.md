The GST Composition Scheme was introduced to significantly reduce the compliance burden for small businesses. Instead of filing monthly GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B returns, composition taxpayers file a simple quarterly statement known as **Form CMP-08**.

However, fewer returns do not mean you can ignore the [Due Dates Calendar](/gst-due-dates). Missing the CMP-08 deadline carries penalties, though the structure differs slightly from regular taxpayers.

## What is Form CMP-08?

Form CMP-08 is a "Statement for payment of self-assessed tax." 
Unlike regular taxpayers who compute complex Input Tax Credit (ITC) equations, composition taxpayers pay a flat percentage of their turnover out of their own pocket (they cannot collect GST from customers, nor can they claim ITC).

- **Traders and Manufacturers**: 1.5% of turnover (0.75% CGST + 0.75% SGST)
- **Restaurants (not serving alcohol)**: 5% of turnover (2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST)
- **Service Providers**: 6% of turnover (3% CGST + 3% SGST)

*(Note: Since you cannot charge GST, you do not need to use a [GST Calculator](/gst-calculator) to add tax to your invoices. Your invoices must be titled "Bill of Supply" instead of "Tax Invoice").*

## The CMP-08 Quarterly Filing Calendar (FY 2025-26 & 2026-27)

CMP-08 must be filed every quarter by the 18th of the month following the quarter's end:

| Quarter | Period Covered | Statutory Due Date | What Needs to be Paid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Q1** | April 1 – June 30 | **July 18** | Self-assessed tax on Q1 turnover |
| **Q2** | July 1 – September 30 | **October 18** | Self-assessed tax on Q2 turnover |
| **Q3** | October 1 – December 31 | **January 18** | Self-assessed tax on Q3 turnover |
| **Q4** | January 1 – March 31 | **April 18** | Self-assessed tax on Q4 turnover |

In addition to quarterly CMP-08, composition dealers must file an annual return in **Form GSTR-4** by **April 30th** following the close of the financial year.

## Penalties and Interest for Late CMP-08

### 1. Late Fees
Historically, the GST portal did not automatically calculate a per-day late fee for filing CMP-08 late (unlike GSTR-3B). However, the late fee is technically absorbed when you file the annual return (GSTR-4). The late fee for GSTR-4 is capped at ₹500 for NIL returns and ₹2,000 for regular returns.

### 2. The 18% Interest
If you miss the 18th deadline and fail to deposit the self-assessed tax, you must pay **18% per annum interest** on the delayed payment amount. Since composition dealers cannot claim ITC, the entire tax amount is paid in cash, meaning the 18% is calculated on the full liability.

## Crucial Restrictions

Remember, as a composition dealer:
1. You cannot make inter-state sales of goods (you can only sell within your state).
2. You cannot supply goods through an e-commerce operator who collects TCS.
3. You must mention "Composition taxable person, not eligible to collect tax on supplies" on every Bill of Supply.

Always perform a [GSTIN Validator](/gstin-validator) check on your suppliers, even though you can't claim ITC, to ensure you are dealing with legitimate businesses. Keeping track of your [GST Due Dates](/gst-due-dates) ensures you enjoy the benefits of the composition scheme without the headache of notices.
