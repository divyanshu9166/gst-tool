Receiving a communication from the GST department can be intimidating, but mistaking a preliminary scrutiny notice for a formal demand order can lead to costly mistakes. The two most common notices received by GST taxpayers are **Form GST ASMT-10** and **Form GST DRC-01**.

While ASMT-10 is an advisory discrepancy notice issued during return scrutiny under **Section 61**, DRC-01 is a formal Show Cause Notice (SCN) issued under **Section 73 or 74** initiating quasi-judicial demand proceedings.

In this guide, we break down the statutory difference between ASMT-10 and DRC-01, the procedural progression from one to the other, and provide exact reply strategies and templates.

## Direct Comparison: ASMT-10 vs DRC-01

| Feature | Form GST ASMT-10 | Form GST DRC-01 |
|---|---|---|
| **Statutory Section** | Section 61 read with Rule 99 | Section 73 (non-fraud) or Section 74 (fraud) read with Rule 142 |
| **Nature of Document** | Scrutiny of Returns notice pointing out discrepancies | Formal Show Cause Notice (SCN) demanding tax, interest, & penalty |
| **Binding Tax Demand?** | No. Only highlights differences (GSTR-3B vs 1 or 2B) | Yes. Quantifies exact tax, interest under Sec 50, and penalties |
| **Statutory Reply Form** | **Form GST ASMT-11** via the GST Portal | **Form GST DRC-06** via the GST Portal |
| **Standard Reply Time** | Maximum **30 days** (or extended time granted by proper officer) | Strictly **30 days** from date of service |
| **Departmental Follow-Up** | If satisfied: **ASMT-12** (Dropped). If not: Initiates Audit/DRC-01A/DRC-01 | If satisfied: **DRC-05** (Dropped). If not: Demand Order in **DRC-07** |
| **Recovery Actions?** | No bank attachment or recovery possible | Failure to reply leads to DRC-07 followed by Section 79 bank attachment |

## What Triggers an ASMT-10 Notice?

The GST Directorate General of Analytics and Risk Management (DGARM) flags accounts automatically based on data algorithms:
1. **Outward Supply Mismatch:** GSTR-1 outward turnover is greater than GSTR-3B reported turnover.
2. **Inward ITC Mismatch:** Input Tax Credit claimed in Table 4(A)(5) of GSTR-3B exceeds eligible ITC available in GSTR-2B.
3. **E-Way Bill vs GSTR-1:** Significant variance between generated E-Way bills and outward supplies declared in returns.
4. **ITC from Cancelled Vendors:** Claiming ITC against suppliers whose GST registration was cancelled retrospectively.
5. **RCM Liability Shortfall:** Higher purchases from unregistered suppliers or GTA freight without corresponding Reverse Charge liability paid in Table 3.1(d).

## Step-by-Step Blueprint: Replying to ASMT-10 in Form ASMT-11

### Step 1: Download the Annexure from the Portal
Log into `gst.gov.in` → Services → User Services → View Additional Notices and Orders. Locate the ASMT-10 order and download the detailed Excel sheet or PDF containing the discrepancy table.

### Step 2: Reconcile with Invoice Level Data
Do not reply with generic denials. Prepare a mathematical reconciliation table comparing:
- Invoice numbers and dates
- Supplier GSTINs and filing dates
- Reasons for timing differences (e.g., ITC claimed in following month, credit notes accounted later).

### Step 3: Draft Your ASMT-11 Submission
Structure your reply under 4 standard legal heads:
- **Reference:** Cite the notice reference number and date.
- **Point-by-Point Clarification:** Address each discrepancy serial number specifically.
- **Statutory & Judicial Precedents:** Refer to relevant CBIC circulars (e.g., Circular No. 183/15/2022-GST for FY 2017-18 / 2018-19 ITC mismatches).
- **Prayer:** Explicitly request the Proper Officer to accept the explanation and issue an acceptance order in **Form GST ASMT-12**.

## Reply Strategy When Moving to DRC-01

If your ASMT-11 was rejected or if the department skipped ASMT-10 and issued DRC-01 directly:
- File your reply in **Form DRC-06** within 30 days.
- Under **Section 75(4) of the CGST Act**, an opportunity of personal hearing is **mandatory** before any adverse decision or order is passed. Always include the statutory hearing prayer:  
  *"Without prejudice to the above submissions, the taxpayer requests a personal hearing before any adverse demand order is finalized."*
- Failure by the officer to grant a personal hearing renders the subsequent DRC-07 order null and void in High Court writ jurisdiction for violation of natural justice.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What if I agree with the discrepancy in ASMT-10?
If you agree that tax was genuinely short-paid, deposit the tax along with interest under Section 50 using **Form GST DRC-03** (selecting "Voluntary" or "Scrutiny"). In your ASMT-11 reply, attach the DRC-03 payment receipt. The officer will close proceedings via ASMT-12 without imposing penal charges.

### Can an officer issue DRC-01 without issuing ASMT-10?
Yes. Multiple High Courts (including Madras and Gujarat High Courts) have held that Section 61 (ASMT-10) is not a mandatory prerequisite for issuing a demand under Section 73 or 74. If the department has independent audit findings or intelligence, they can directly issue DRC-01A and DRC-01.
