Selling products online via marketplaces like Amazon India, Flipkart, Meesho, or Nykaa offers unprecedented reach. However, e-commerce sellers are subject to the strictest indirect tax oversight in India.

Unlike offline brick-and-mortar retailers who file basic monthly returns, online sellers must account for **Tax Collected at Source (TCS) under Section 52**, reconcile marketplace settlement sheets against GSTR-1, claim credit for customer returns across state borders, and obtain multi-state GSTINs for **Fulfilled by Amazon (FBA)** or Flipkart Advantage warehouses.

In this guide, we provide a complete compliance checklist and monthly calendar designed specifically for Indian e-commerce merchants.

## The 5-Point E-Commerce Compliance Checklist

### 1. Section 52 TCS Deduction & Claiming Workflow
Under **Section 52 of the CGST Act**, e-commerce operators deduct **Tax Collected at Source (TCS)** at the rate of **0.5% (0.25% CGST + 0.25% SGST) or 0.5% IGST** on the net value of taxable supplies made through their platform.
- **How to Recover Your Money:** Marketplace TCS does not sit in a vacuum. Once Amazon/Flipkart files Form GSTR-8 on the 10th:
  1. Log in to `gst.gov.in`.
  2. Navigate to **Services** → **Returns** → **TDS and TCS Credit Received**.
  3. Review the auto-drafted net sales and TCS deductions, and click **Accept**.
  4. The accepted TCS amount is **instantly credited to your Electronic Cash Ledger**, which you can use directly to pay your GSTR-3B taxes or claim as a cash refund!

### 2. Fulfilled by Amazon (FBA) & Principal Place of Business (PPoB / APoB)
If you enroll in Amazon FBA or Flipkart Smart Fulfillment to offer Prime 1-day delivery, your inventory is physically stored in marketplace fulfillment centers located in different states (e.g., Haryana, Karnataka, Telangana, Maharashtra).
- **Statutory Rule:** You **cannot** store inventory in another state without obtaining a GST registration in that state.
- **The Process:** You must obtain a separate GSTIN in each state where an FBA warehouse is located, registering Amazon/Flipkart's warehouse as an **Additional Place of Business (APoB)** using their formal consent letter and municipal trade license.

### 3. State-Wise B2C Reporting (Table 7 of GSTR-1)
When selling to end-consumers across India (B2C):
- Every sale must be reported based on the **Place of Supply (POS)**, which is the delivery location of the customer.
- Under **Table 7 of GSTR-1**, you must disclose gross inter-state supplies state-by-state at respective tax rates (e.g., sales to Uttar Pradesh at 18%, sales to Tamil Nadu at 12%).

### 4. Handling High Customer Return Rates (RTO)
High Return-to-Origin (RTO) and customer return rates (often 20% to 35% in fashion and footwear) create significant accounting headaches:
- When a customer returns an item, the marketplace issues a negative credit adjustment in their settlement report.
- **GST Reconciliation:** In your GSTR-1, outward sales must be reported **net of sales returns / credit notes**. If your credit notes in a particular state exceed fresh sales in a given month, the net negative figure can be carried forward to subsequent months.

### 5. Reconciling Marketplace Inward Fee Invoices
Amazon and Flipkart issue monthly B2B tax invoices to you for:
- Marketplace Referral Fees & Closing Fees
- FBA Pick & Pack Fees and Storage Fees
- In-platform Advertising / Sponsored Product Ads
- Logistics & Shipping Charges  
Ensure these inward invoices appear in your **GSTR-2B** so your business claims the full **18% Input Tax Credit** on these platform expenses.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Can small e-commerce sellers with turnover below ₹40 Lakhs sell without GST?
Under recent GST Council relaxations, intra-state sellers with turnover below the threshold (₹40L for goods) can sell goods through e-commerce operators without mandatory GST registration, provided they obtain an **Enrolment Number** on the GST portal. However, these unregistered sellers **cannot make inter-state supplies** and cannot store goods in out-of-state FBA warehouses.

### What should I do if Amazon's GSTR-8 figures do not match my sales register?
If there is a variance between Amazon's reported GSTR-8 sales and your internal sales reports (often due to transit timing differences at month-end), accept the undisputed records on the portal and claim the variance in the subsequent month's reconciliation.
