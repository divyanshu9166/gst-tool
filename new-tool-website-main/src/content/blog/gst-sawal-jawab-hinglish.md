GST ke rules English mein milte hain, lekin chhote business ke practical sawal aksar Hinglish mein hote hain. Neeche common situations ko simple language mein samjhaya hai. Har business ka return type, turnover, transaction aur state alag ho sakta hai, isliye final filing se pehle GST portal par apni applicable details check karein.

## “GST return ki due date kya hoti hai?”

Ek hi due date sab taxpayers ke liye nahi hoti. GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, CMP-08 aur QRMP filers ke schedules alag hain; tax period aur extensions bhi matter karte hain. Apne GST portal ke return dashboard aur [GST Due Dates Calendar](/gst-due-dates) se current period ka date confirm karein. Kisi purane calendar ko bina year aur notification check kiye follow na karein.

## “Baar-baar GST return late file karun to kya hoga?”

Late filing par applicable late fee lag sakti hai, aur unpaid tax par interest ka issue alag se ho sakta hai. Long delay se notices ya registration-related action ka risk facts aur applicable provisions par depend karta hai; har case mein automatic cancellation ya account freeze assume na karein. Apni liability estimate karne ke liye [GST Late Fee Calculator](/late-fee-calculator) dekhein aur relevant notice aaye to uski deadline miss na karein.

## “Nil return hai, phir bhi file karna padega?”

Nil liability ka matlab har baar return ki requirement khatam hona nahi hota. Agar aapka return period active hai, to applicable NIL return file karna pad sakta hai. GST portal par period aur form verify karein; nil-return late fee ke liye hamari [Nil Return Late Fee Guide](/blog/late-fee-for-nil-returns) dekhein.

## “GSTR-2B mein purchase invoice nahi dikh rahi—ITC le sakta hoon?”

Sirf invoice milne se ITC automatic eligible nahi ho jata. Supplier reporting, Section 16 ki conditions, invoice details, receipt of supply, payment rules aur blocked-credit restrictions check karein. Pehle supplier se invoice reporting correct karwayein aur apni books ko GSTR-2B se reconcile karein. [GSTR-2B mismatch guide](/blog/gstr-2b-mismatch-itc-blocked-fix-guide) mein reconciliation steps hain.

## “GSTIN valid dikh raha hai, iska matlab business genuine hai?”

Nahi. GSTIN ka format/checksum valid hona aur portal par registration status active hona alag checks hain; dono se business ki har representation prove nahi hoti. Invoice, supplier details, status aur transaction evidence independently verify karein. [GSTIN Validator](/gstin-validator) par format check karein aur zarurat par official GST portal se live status confirm karein.

## “Mujhe legal fees par RCM bharna hai kya?”

Har lawyer invoice par nahi. Advocate ya law firm ki notified legal service, recipient ka business-entity status, taxable territory aur applicable notification check karni hoti hai. Covered legal-services RCM ka legal basis aam taur par Section 9(3) aur Notification No. 13/2017-Central Tax (Rate) hota hai. [Legal-services RCM guide](/blog/gst-reverse-charge-legal-services-2026) padhein aur amount ka estimate [RCM Calculator](/rcm-calculator) se karein.

## “Marketplace ne paise kaate hain—kya woh sab GST hai?”

Zaroori nahi. Marketplace settlement mein income-tax TDS, GST TCS, platform fees, refunds aur doosre adjustments alag lines mein ho sakte hain. Har deduction ko uske statement aur tax law ke hisaab se reconcile karein; GST TCS credit cash ledger mein, income-tax TDS income-tax records mein track hota hai. [Marketplace TDS vs GST TDS/TCS guide](/blog/marketplace-tds-gst-tds-tcs-ecommerce-2026) dekhein.

## “Customer ne goods return kiye—credit note kaise banau?”

Supplier original invoice ke against Section 34 GST credit note issue kar sakta hai where the law permits; tax liability reduce karne ke liye deadline aur other conditions follow karne hote hain. Agar output GST adjust karne ki legal conditions puri nahi hoti, commercial credit note ka treatment alag ho sakta hai. [Credit/debit note rules guide](/blog/gst-credit-debit-note-rules-2026) aur [Credit/Debit Note Generator](/credit-debit-note-generator) dekhein.

## “GST invoice mein HSN code galat ho to kya karun?”

Pehle goods ya service ki sahi classification identify karein; description dekhkar code guess na karein. Invoice/return mein galti ka correction document aur reporting period par depend karega. [HSN/SAC Lookup](/hsn-code-lookup) ek research starting point hai—final classification aur rate ko current official schedule se verify karein.

## “GST notice aaya hai—kya turant payment kar doon?”

Pehle notice ka form/reference number, section, tax period, allegation, reply deadline aur portal par available document verify karein. Scrutiny communication aur demand/show-cause notice alag stages ho sakte hain. Deadline note karein, evidence collect karein aur amount ya reply position decide karne se pehle notice ko samjhein. [GST Notices Hub](/gst-notices) par common notice types aur response resources hain.

## “E-invoice banayi hai, ab return file nahi karna?”

E-invoice reporting aur GST return filing alag compliance steps hain. Applicable e-invoice obligation ho to invoice ka IRN/QR workflow follow karein, aur apne prescribed returns bhi file karein. [E-Invoice Eligibility Checker](/e-invoice-eligibility-checker) se initial check karein; current turnover threshold, exclusions aur notifications verify karna zaroori hai.
