## When Does a YouTuber Need GST Registration?

Under **Section 22 of the CGST Act**, every service provider must register for GST if their aggregate turnover in a financial year exceeds **₹20 Lakhs** (or **₹10 Lakhs** if operating from Special Category States like Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland, Tripura).

### The "Zero-Rated" Turnover Trap:
A common mistake among creators is assuming that because foreign AdSense payments are not taxed (0% GST), they don't count toward the ₹20 Lakh threshold.  
**This is legally incorrect.** Under Section 2(6) and Section 2(47), aggregate turnover includes **all taxable, exempt, and zero-rated export supplies**.  
- If you earn ₹18 Lakhs from YouTube AdSense (foreign) and ₹3 Lakhs from domestic brand collaborations, your total turnover is **₹21 Lakhs**.
- You **must register for GST**, even though the majority of your earnings are zero-rated.

## Google AdSense Revenue: Zero-Rated Export under LUT

When you monetize a YouTube channel, your contract is executed with **Google Ireland Limited** or **Google LLC (USA)**. Because the recipient of service is located outside India and payment arrives in foreign exchange (via Wire Transfer/SWIFT), this transaction qualifies as an **Export of Service under Section 2(6) of the IGST Act**.

### How to File AdSense Income:
1. **File Annual LUT (Form GST RFD-11):** Submit a Letter of Undertaking on the GST portal before April 1st of each financial year. This allows you to export services without paying 18% IGST upfront.
2. **Issue Self-Invoices:** Create a monthly invoice addressed to Google Ireland Limited citing your GSTIN, Google's Dublin address, the SAC code **998365** (Internet advertising services), and the statutory LUT declaration:  
   *"Supply meant for export under Letter of Undertaking without payment of Integrated Tax (IGST)."*
3. **Collect FIRC / BRC:** Download Foreign Inward Remittance Certificates (FIRC) or e-BRCs from your receiving bank (HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Wise, etc.) as legal proof of realization in convertible foreign exchange.
4. **Report in GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B:** Report your AdSense earnings in **Table 6A of GSTR-1** (Exports without payment of tax) and **Table 3.1(b) of GSTR-3B**.

## Brand Sponsorships, Affiliate Marketing & Merchandise

Not all creator revenue is export income. Here is the taxation breakdown across different revenue streams:

| Revenue Stream | Payer Location | GST SAC Code | GST Rate | ITC Availability |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **YouTube AdSense** | Abroad (Google Ireland) | 998365 | **0%** (under LUT) | Eligible for Refund |
| **Brand Sponsorships (Indian Brands)** | India | 998361 / 998365 | **18%** (CGST 9% + SGST 9% or IGST 18%) | Regular domestic ITC |
| **Brand Sponsorships (Global Brands)** | Abroad | 998365 | **0%** (under LUT) | Eligible for Refund |
| **Affiliate Commissions (Amazon Associates IN)** | India | 998361 | **18%** | Regular domestic ITC |
| **Merchandise / E-Commerce Sales** | India | Relevant HSN | 5% / 12% / 18% | Regular domestic ITC |
| **Brand Barter Deals (Free Products for Review)** | India | 998361 | **18%** on Fair Market Value | Section 15 Valuation Rules |

> **Warning on Barter Deals:**  
> If an Indian phone or automobile brand sends you a ₹1,00,000 device in exchange for an unboxing or sponsored reel, tax authorities consider this a barter transaction under Section 7. The fair market value of the item is taxable at 18%.

## Massive Tax Savings: Claiming ITC on Creator Gear

Once you are registered under GST, you can claim 100% **Input Tax Credit (ITC)** on the capital equipment and professional services used to run your creator business:

- **Cameras, Lenses & Microphones:** 18% GST credited.
- **MacBooks, Editing PCs & Monitors:** 18% GST credited.
- **Studio Lights & Acoustic Panels:** 18% GST credited.
- **Broadband Internet & Mobile Bills:** 18% GST credited (under your trade name/GSTIN).
- **Video Editing Software (Adobe CC, Final Cut):** 18% GST credited.
- **Studio Rent:** 18% GST credited if rented under a commercial lease agreement.

Because creators exporting through AdSense collect 0% tax but pay 18% tax on their production gear, they accumulate significant credits. You can file **Form GST RFD-01 under Rule 89(4)** to receive this accumulated ITC as a **cash refund directly in your bank account**!

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What happens if I receive AdSense income without an LUT?
If you fail to furnish an LUT in Form RFD-11, the tax department can legally demand **18% IGST on your entire YouTube revenue** along with 18% interest under Section 50. Always ensure your LUT is renewed annually on the GST portal before April 1st.

### Can a YouTuber opt for the GST Composition Scheme?
Generally, no. Section 10(2)(d) of the CGST Act strictly prohibits composition dealers from making **inter-state or export supplies**. Since Google AdSense and global sponsorships are exports (inter-state under Section 7(5) of the IGST Act), creators cannot opt for the Composition Scheme.
