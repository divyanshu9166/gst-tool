House Rent Allowance (HRA) is one of the most effective tax-saving components for salaried employees living in rented accommodation across India. However, many taxpayers either miscalculate their exemption or fail to understand how it interacts with the **New Tax Regime** vs. **Old Tax Regime**.

Under **Section 10(13A)** of the Income Tax Act, 1961, read with **Rule 2A** of the Income Tax Rules, HRA exemption is not an arbitrary lump-sum allowance. It is calculated using a strict three-part statutory formula.

In this comprehensive guide, we unpack the exact formula, statutory conditions, documentation requirements, and how to maximize your tax deductions.

## The Statutory 3-Part Formula under Rule 2A

The tax-exempt portion of your HRA is the **lowest** of the following three amounts:

1. **Actual HRA received** from your employer during the financial year.
2. **Actual rent paid minus 10% of salary** (Basic + Dearness Allowance forming part of retirement benefits).
3. **50% of salary** if you reside in a metro city (Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai) OR **40% of salary** if you reside in any non-metro city (e.g., Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad, Gurugram).

> **Important Definition of "Salary":**  
> For HRA calculation under Rule 2A, "Salary" strictly means:  
> **Basic Salary + Dearness Allowance (DA)** (only if considered for retirement benefits) + **Fixed percentage turnover commissions**.  
> Special allowances, performance bonuses, LTA, and medical reimbursements are strictly excluded.

## Practical Case Study: Metro vs Non-Metro

Let us analyze a concrete numerical example for FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27:

- **Employee**: Rahul
- **Location**: Mumbai (Metro - 50%)
- **Basic Salary**: ₹80,000 / month (₹9,60,000 / year)
- **HRA Received**: ₹35,000 / month (₹4,20,000 / year)
- **Actual Rent Paid**: ₹30,000 / month (₹3,60,000 / year)

### Step-by-Step Rule 2A Evaluation:

1. **Actual HRA received**: ₹4,20,000
2. **Rent paid minus 10% of salary**:  
   ₹3,60,000 - 10% of ₹9,60,000  
   = ₹3,60,000 - ₹96,000  
   = **₹2,64,000**
3. **50% of salary (Metro city)**:  
   50% of ₹9,60,000 = **₹4,80,000**

**Exempt HRA**: The lowest among [₹4,20,000, ₹2,64,000, ₹4,80,000] is **₹2,64,000**.  
**Taxable HRA**: ₹4,20,000 - ₹2,64,000 = **₹1,56,000** (added to taxable salary income).

## Crucial Statutory Rules to Remember

### 1. Landlord's PAN is Mandatory Above ₹1,00,000 Annual Rent
Under CBDT circulars, if your annual rent exceeds **₹1,00,000** (₹8,333/month), furnishing the landlord's Permanent Account Number (PAN) or Aadhaar to your employer is legally mandatory for TDS form 16 credit. If your landlord does not have a PAN, a signed declaration in Form 60 must be collected.

### 2. Can You Pay Rent to Parents?
Yes. If you live with your parents in a property registered exclusively in their names, you can legally pay them rent, collect monthly rent receipts, and claim HRA exemption.  
*Key Compliances:*
- The parents must declare this rental income in their respective Income Tax Returns (ITR).
- Execute a formal rental agreement and transfer rent via banking channels (NEFT/UPI).
- You cannot pay rent to your spouse, as husband and wife are legally deemed to share a common marital residence.

### 3. Can You Claim Both HRA and Home Loan Interest?
Yes, under Section 24(b) (Home loan interest up to ₹2,00,000) and Section 10(13A) (HRA), you can claim both deductions simultaneously if:
- Your self-owned home is in another city due to your employment location.
- Your own house is under construction or rented out to third parties.

## HRA in New Tax Regime vs Old Tax Regime

| Parameter | Old Tax Regime | New Tax Regime (Sec 115BAC) |
|---|---|---|
| **HRA Exemption** | **Allowed** under Section 10(13A) | **NOT Allowed** (Fully Taxable) |
| **Standard Deduction** | ₹50,000 | ₹75,000 (FY 2025-26 onwards) |
| **Tax Slabs** | Higher slab rates (up to 30% from ₹10L) | Concessional lower slabs (Rebate up to ₹7.75L) |
| **80C & 80D Deductions** | Allowed (₹1.5L + ₹25k-₹1L) | Not Allowed |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### What if my employer does not show HRA in Form 16?
If your employer failed to consider your rent receipts during TDS processing, you can directly claim the Section 10(13A) exemption while filing your annual ITR (ITR-1 or ITR-2) before July 31st. Ensure you retain your rental agreement, bank statements, and rent receipts in case of departmental scrutiny.

### Is Bengaluru or Hyderabad considered a Metro for HRA?
For the purpose of Income Tax Rule 2A, **only 4 cities** are classified as metros: Mumbai, Kolkata, Delhi, and Chennai. Even though Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Pune are mega IT hubs, they fall under the **40% non-metro salary limit**.

### What happens if I change cities or jobs mid-year?
HRA calculation must be computed on a **monthly or period-by-period basis** whenever there is a change in salary, rent paid, city of residence, or employment. An annualized calculation will result in errors.
